# Exam and practice room

Applies to `/student/quiz/:quizId` and `/student/practice/:sessionId/room`.

- Preserve the focused dark exam canvas and shared question card; keep the answer area as the visual priority.
- Use blue for the selected answer and navigation focus, teal only for neutral feature accents, green for correct, red for incorrect, and amber for marked questions.
- Keep the question text at a comfortable reading width and preserve line wrapping for long Vietnamese text and math.
- Make the question navigator collapsible without hiding the restore control; preserve the current question state when toggled.
- Keep primary controls at least 44px high, with visible keyboard focus and adequate space between touch targets.
- Avoid motion that distracts during timed exams; honor reduced-motion preferences.
